Market Overview

Gold has delivered exceptional returns in 2025, rising 82.3% year-to-date to reach ₹14,000 per gram. This explosive rally reflects structural support from central bank accumulation (634 MT in first 9 months), geopolitical uncertainty (Russia-Ukraine, Taiwan, Middle East), weakening real interest rates (-0.1% to -0.2%), and accelerating de-dollarization trends. The INR gold price movement from ₹7,678/gram (start of 2025) to ₹14,000/gram has been driven by: (1) International gold price appreciation of approximately 50% in USD terms, and (2) Rupee depreciation from 83.5 to 89.81 (7.5% weakness) adding significant INR price tailwind.

Over the past five years (2020-2025), gold has delivered a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 22.8% in INR terms, substantially outperforming traditional fixed deposits (6-7% CAGR) and matching equity market returns with significantly lower volatility (12-15% vs 18-22% for equities). This performance underscores gold's dual role as both a wealth preservation tool during crises and a tactical investment asset for portfolio diversification. The structural factors supporting gold prices remain intact for the medium to long term.
